Isochrone fit

Fits age, mass, radius, Teff, logg, and Fe/H against MIST isochrones (isochrones2's SingleStarModel), using Gaia BP/G/RP and 2MASS J/H/Ks photometry plus parallax from this target's reference table (Target page). Broadband photometry alone is weakly informative for a main-sequence dwarf's age; checking the optional spectroscopic constraints below can help.

±

Matches notebooks/ext_code_isochrones.ipynb's mod.set_prior(...) -- restricts age to a flat prior over log₁₀(age/yr), and AV extinction to a flat prior in mag. EEP (evolutionary stage) isn't touched by the notebook but is still one of the fit's free parameters, so it's editable here too -- default is the full MIST grid (no restriction).

⚠
Gyrochronology validity warning

A significant fraction of the gyro-interp posterior lies below the youngest calibration cluster (~80 Myr, Bouma+2023). Gyrochronology ages in this regime are extrapolations and should not be treated as reliable. The joint age shown below is biased by this invalid gyro constraint.

Synthesis
Age posterior comparison

The joint posterior is the normalized product of the method PDFs on a shared age grid, assuming statistical independence between methods. Because BAFFLES and EAGLES both age the star from lithium depletion, the joint combines at most one lithium-based posterior (EAGLES) and BAFFLES contributes only its chromospheric-activity component, so the lithium constraint is not double-counted.
